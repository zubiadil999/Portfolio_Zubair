ABOUT ME

Your child learns directly from me — every class.

ZUBAIR MUGHAL

I'm not a company. I'm not a platform. I'm the person who actually teaches your child.

I've spent the last few years teaching coding as an instructor, working with beginners of all ages. Now I'm building an independent teaching practice so I can go deeper with fewer students, instead of teaching at scale.

My main focus is helping kids ages 8–12 and complete beginners understand programming from the ground up — without pressure, without jargon, and without assuming they already know anything.

Classes are available in both 1:1 and group formats, so every student learns in the format that fits them best. I don't sell pre-recorded courses — every session is live, and I'm the one running it.

My teaching style is simple: break difficult concepts into understandable pieces, write the code together, practice what we learn, and then build something real. It's not about memorizing syntax — it's about understanding how things work and building the confidence to try things independently.
