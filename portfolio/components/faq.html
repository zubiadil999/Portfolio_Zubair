FAQ

Questions parents usually ask.

Everything you need to know about live online classes, who they are for, and how learning works.

What age group do you teach? +

My primary focus is kids ages 8–12. I also teach complete beginners of other ages, including older school students and college beginners.

Are the classes completely online? +

Yes. All classes are conducted live online, so your child can learn from home.

Does my child need previous coding experience? +

No. Most of my students are complete beginners. We start from the basics and build up step-by-step.

Are classes 1:1 or group-based? +

Both are available. 1:1 classes give your child completely personalized attention. Group classes let them learn alongside other students at a similar level. We can help you choose during the trial class.

Do you sell recorded courses? +

No. I focus entirely on live teaching and direct interaction — I personally teach every class, rather than selling pre-recorded videos.

What will we do during the classes? +

Classes combine explanations, live coding, guided practice and hands-on projects, so your child applies what they learn instead of just watching.

How does the trial class work? +

You book a time that works for you, we hop on a live call, and I run a real class so you and your child can see how I teach. Afterward, we talk about your child's goals and the best next step — no pressure to continue.

Will I be able to track my child's progress? +

Yes. As the instructor, I communicate directly with parents about how each student is progressing — there's no separate account manager or support team in between.

How are classes scheduled? +

We agree on a schedule that works for your family after the trial class. Tell me your preferred days and times in the contact form and I'll confirm availability.

What does my child need for a class? +

A computer or laptop with a stable internet connection is enough to get started. I'll guide you through any specific setup needed before the first class.

How much do classes cost? +

Pricing depends on whether you choose 1:1 or group classes and how often you'd like to meet. The trial class is the easiest way to discuss pricing that fits your goals.

What is your cancellation or refund policy? +

Reach out directly and we'll sort out rescheduling or cancellation — this section will be updated with a full policy soon.
